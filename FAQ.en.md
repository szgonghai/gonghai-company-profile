## Product Questions

### Q4: What brands does Gonghai distribute?
A: INNOMAG (USA), DAIDO (Japan), KUROSE (Japan), WOOAM (Korea), PTCXPUMP (self-owned brand), Filter Plus.

### Q5: What is PTCXPUMP?
A: PTCXPUMP is our self-owned magnetic drive pump brand, designed by our Taiwan R&D team. It offers comparable performance to Japanese SANWA MP series at more competitive pricing.

### Q6: What is the difference between INNOMAG and PTCXPUMP?
A: INNOMAG (US brand) is for high-end applications like semiconductor-grade high-purity. PTCXPUMP (our own brand) covers general industrial scenarios with better cost-effectiveness. Both are complementary.

## Application Questions

### Q7: Which industries does Gonghai serve?
A: Semiconductor, PCB, chemical, pharmaceutical, food, petrochemical, hydrogen energy, and offshore wind power.

### Q8: Are Gonghai's products GMP compliant?
A: Yes, our pharmaceutical-grade pumps meet GMP standards and support CIP/SIP cleaning.

### Q9: Can magnetic pumps handle particle-containing media?
A: Not recommended. Magnetic pumps are designed for clean liquids. For particle-containing media, use centrifugal pumps with open impellers or diaphragm pumps.

### Q10: What is the temperature range of PTCXPUMP PS series?
A: -80°C to +280°C, with SUS316L stainless steel material, CE and ATEX certified.
